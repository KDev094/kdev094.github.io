Led the development of an intelligent News Aggregator that combines traditional NLP and machine learning techniques with Generative AI to process and organize large volumes of news information.

The solution brought together different generations of NLP techniques, using conventional models where deterministic classification and extraction were effective while introducing generative capabilities for tasks requiring richer language understanding and interpretation.

The project reinforced an important architectural principle in my work: an effective AI system does not need to solve every problem with the same model. Combining specialized classical models with modern generative models can provide a better balance of accuracy, efficiency, explainability, and computational cost.
