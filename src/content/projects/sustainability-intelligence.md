Designed an AI-enabled solution concept for creating a unified and governed source of sustainability performance information across organizational KPIs and supplier data.

The platform was designed to consolidate fragmented sustainability information, surface KPI and supplier performance, identify areas requiring attention, and use AI to generate recommendations for improving sustainability outcomes.

The project provided an opportunity to explore how Agentic AI can operate over governed enterprise data while preserving traceability and ensuring that generated recommendations remain grounded in measurable sustainability indicators.
