Built robust Name and Address Parsers for extracting structured information from noisy and inconsistently formatted textual data.

The solution addressed a common enterprise data challenge: semantically equivalent information can appear in many different textual forms because of formatting differences, abbreviations, missing fields, spelling variations, and other data-quality issues.

The project strengthened my understanding of information extraction, normalization, sequence-based NLP, and the importance of designing machine learning systems around imperfect real-world data rather than assuming clean and consistently formatted inputs.
