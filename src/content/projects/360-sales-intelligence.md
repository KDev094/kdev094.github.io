Designed and led the development of a 360° Sales Intelligence capability that combines internal enterprise information with external web intelligence to provide sales teams with a more complete understanding of their accounts.

The platform builds comprehensive restaurant profiles covering digital presence, customer engagement, menu and product offerings, purchasing behavior, sales trends, and market positioning. AI-driven analysis identifies cross-sell, up-sell, retention, and account-growth opportunities and translates complex information into actionable recommendations for Sales Representatives.

The project deepened my understanding of how internal structured data, unstructured external information, LLMs, and autonomous agents can work together to create contextual intelligence that supports real-world sales decisions.
