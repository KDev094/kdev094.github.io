Designed an Agentic AI–based solution for automatically interpreting balance and financial data and converting complex numerical information into concise, decision-ready business commentary.

The solution concept focused on moving beyond simple metric reporting by identifying significant movements, interpreting variances, connecting related financial signals, and generating contextual explanations that help business users understand what changed and where attention may be required.

The work strengthened my thinking around grounded AI-generated commentary, analytical reasoning over structured data, and the importance of combining deterministic financial calculations with generative capabilities rather than relying on an LLM for numerical reasoning alone.
