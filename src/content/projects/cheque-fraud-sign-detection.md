Led the development of computer vision solutions for banking workflows focused on cheque fraud detection and offline handwritten signature verification.

The work involved building and training deep neural networks capable of learning discriminative visual representations from cheque and signature images, allowing the systems to identify suspicious patterns and determine similarity between handwritten signatures.

Beyond model development, the project covered the broader machine learning lifecycle—from data collection and preparation through experimentation, training, evaluation, deployment, and production maintenance.

The project strengthened my understanding of applying deep learning to high-precision financial workflows where model performance, reliability, and production scalability are equally important.
