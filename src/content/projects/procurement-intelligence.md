Designed and led the enterprise deployment of an Agentic AI platform that ingests, validates, classifies, and analyzes procurement data to provide actionable intelligence across spend, supplier performance, sourcing opportunities, and procurement exceptions.

The solution uses intelligent orchestration and autonomous agents to identify cost-saving opportunities, supplier risks, and root causes behind procurement variances. Its architecture was designed for production-scale usage with caching and LLM optimization strategies that reduce latency, token consumption, and compute costs while maintaining enterprise security, governance, and scalability.

The project strengthened my experience in taking Agentic AI systems beyond prototypes and designing them around real enterprise workflows, operational constraints, and measurable business outcomes.
