Developed a deep learning–based OCR solution for extracting information from documents containing both machine-printed and handwritten text.

The project required treating document understanding as more than simple character recognition. Different document structures, handwriting variations, image quality, and visual layouts introduced challenges across preprocessing, text localization, recognition, and structured information extraction.

The work strengthened my understanding of document AI pipelines and how computer vision and text-processing techniques can be combined to transform unstructured document images into information that downstream enterprise systems can consume.
