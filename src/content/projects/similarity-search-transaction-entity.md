Built a similarity search engine for retrieving the most probable entities associated with transactions when the available information was noisy, incomplete, or inconsistently represented.

The system focused on matching imperfect transaction information against known entities and ranking potential matches according to similarity, allowing downstream workflows to work with probable entity associations instead of relying exclusively on exact matching.

The project significantly shaped my thinking about search and entity resolution. Real-world retrieval is rarely about finding an identical record; it is about representing information in a form where semantically or structurally similar entities can be discovered and ranked despite noise in the underlying data.
